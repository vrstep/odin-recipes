Curry

Description

Curry is a popular dish that originated in India and has become a staple in many cuisines around the world. It typically consists of a spiced sauce with meat, vegetables, or legumes, served over rice or with bread. There are many variations of curry, each with its own unique blend of spices and ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 inch piece of ginger
  • 1 lb meat or vegetables
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 1 cup water
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Cilantro (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the onion until translucent.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger to the pan and cook until fragrant.
  3. Add the meat or vegetables to the pan and cook until browned.
  4. Stir in the curry powder and cook for a few minutes to toast the spices.
  5. Pour in the coconut milk and water, and season with salt and pepper.
  6. Simmer the curry until the meat is cooked through or the vegetables are tender.
  7. Garnish with cilantro before serving, if desired.
