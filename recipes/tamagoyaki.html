Tamagoyaki

Description

Tamagoyaki is a Japanese rolled omelette that is made by rolling together thin layers of seasoned egg in a rectangular pan. It is a popular dish in Japan and is often served for breakfast or as a side dish. Tamagoyaki is sweet and savory, with a light and fluffy texture that makes it a favorite among both children and adults.

Ingredients

  • 4 eggs
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and add the sugar, soy sauce, mirin, and salt.
  2. Whisk the mixture until well combined.
  3. Heat a rectangular pan over medium heat and brush with oil.
  4. Pour a thin layer of the egg mixture into the pan and tilt to spread evenly.
  5. When the egg is set but still slightly runny, roll it up from one end of the pan to the other.
  6. Push the rolled egg to the other end of the pan and brush with oil.
  7. Pour another thin layer of the egg mixture into the pan, lifting the rolled egg to let the new layer flow underneath.
  8. When the new layer is set but still slightly runny, roll it up with the existing egg roll.
  9. Repeat the process until all the egg mixture is used.
  10. Remove the rolled omelette from the pan and let it cool slightly before slicing into rounds.
