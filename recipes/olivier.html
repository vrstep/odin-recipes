Olivier salad

Description

Olivier salad is a traditional salad dish from Russia. It is also known as Russian salad. It is made with diced vegetables, meat, and mayonnaise.

Ingredients

  • 4 potatoes
  • 4 carrots
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 can of peas
  • 1 can of corn
  • 1 can of pickles
  • 1 can of olives
  • 1 can of sausages
  • Mayonnaise

Steps

  1. Boil the potatoes, carrots, and eggs.
  2. Dice the potatoes, carrots, eggs, pickles, olives, and sausages.
  3. Combine all the diced ingredients in a large bowl.
  4. Add the peas and corn to the bowl.
  5. Add mayonnaise to the bowl and mix everything together.
  6. Refrigerate the salad for at least an hour before serving.
