Omurice

Description

Omurice is a popular Japanese dish that consists of an omelette with fried rice and ketchup. It is a simple and delicious meal that is enjoyed by people of all ages.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of cooked rice
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1/2 carrot
  • 1/2 chicken breast
  • 2 eggs
  • Ketchup
  • Soy sauce
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Oil

Steps

  1. Chop the onion, carrot, and chicken breast into small pieces.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and sauté the onion until translucent.
  3. Add the carrot and chicken to the pan and cook until the chicken is no longer pink.
  4. Add the rice to the pan and stir-fry until heated through.
  5. Season the rice with soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
  6. Transfer the rice to a plate and shape it into an oval mound.
  7. Beat the eggs in a bowl and season with salt.
  8. Heat oil in a pan and pour in the beaten eggs to make an omelette.
  9. Place the omelette on top of the rice mound and shape it to cover the rice.
  10. Drizzle ketchup on top of the omelette.
